# Homepage demo

The homepage renders one wide `<DemoFrame />` under the hero. Until the file
exists it shows a placeholder naming this path.

    public/demo/loop.mp4

## What it should show

The whole loop in one take, because that is the claim the homepage makes:

1. **Design** - a component appearing on the Genesis canvas.
2. **Run** - `brain.py` starting and answering a request through a Receptor.
3. **Observe** - the same components lighting up in Prism as the Signals fire.

The single most valuable frame in the whole recording is the moment a viewer
can see that the node they placed in Genesis is the node now pulsing in Prism -
they share a silhouette on purpose. If you have to cut anything, cut the
middle step, not that one.

Keep it short. This sits above the fold and loops forever - 10-20 seconds,
no narration, no cursor hunting. 16:9, under ~8 MB. The frame here is wider
than the rest of the site (1000px vs 880px), so a crisp capture is worth it.
